# DineEasy User Manual

## 1. Customer Digital Menu
1. Scan the QR code placed on your restaurant table.
2. Verify your locked Table Number at the top of the screen and choose your preferred language (English, Khmer, Chinese, Vietnamese, Japanese, Korean, French, or any admin-added language).
3. Browse categories or search for dishes, tap a dish to customize size, spice/sugar/ice levels, or add-ons, and tap **Add to Cart**.
4. Open **Cart**, add any order notes, and tap **Order Now**.
5. Track your order status live in **Orders** (`Order Placed` → `Confirmed` → `Preparing` → `Ready` → `Delivered`).
6. When finished dining, tap **Request Checkout**, pay via Static KHQR or cash with staff, and download your digital PDF receipt.

## 2. Staff Order Management & Checkout
1. Sign in at `/staff` (`sokha` / `staff123`).
2. Watch the **Orders** queue for incoming customer orders and tap **Confirm Order** to route tickets to the kitchen.
3. When the kitchen marks an order **Ready**, deliver it to the table and tap **Mark Served**.
4. Open **Checkout** for a table session to review the consolidated bill across all orders in that session, apply discounts if needed, and display **Static KHQR** or select **Cash**.
5. After verifying incoming funds in the restaurant's banking app, check the verification confirmation and tap **Mark as Paid & Issue Receipt**.

## 3. Chef Kitchen Display System (KDS)
1. Sign in at `/chef` (`chef` / `chef123`) on a kitchen tablet.
2. Confirmed orders appear oldest-first with elapsed prep timers and audible alerts.
3. Tap **Start Cooking** when preparation begins and **Mark Ready** when the dish is ready for pickup at the pass.

## 4. Admin Dashboard
1. Sign in at `/admin` (`admin` / `admin123`).
2. Monitor daily/weekly sales, category revenue, and top-selling dishes on the Dashboard.
3. Manage dishes, modifiers, categories, table QR codes, staff accounts, reports, expenses, translations, and KHQR/currency settings.
